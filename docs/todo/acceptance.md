# The acceptance in a VM

The acceptance page of the customer is a claude.ai page (its address is with the agent that keeps
it): every part is a list of steps with what each should show, run in a VM of Windows 11 on the
archives of a build, and the customer marks every step (pass, fail, skip, with a note).

Accepted: every part up to Q of 0.22.1.

Waiting for the marks of the customer:

- R to V of 0.22.1;
- W to Z and AA to AF of 0.24.0 and 0.25.0;
- AG to AL of 0.26.0 (the command line, the icons of the menus, the comparison of catalogs, the
  Group Policy editor, the catalog by mechanism, wwlite);
- AM to AR of 0.27.0 (the region of the default user, the widths of the editors, text sizes up to
  36, the menu Catalogs, wwlite in the browser, the presentation).

The parts of wedit (W to Z of 0.24.0) and the versions of wview now belong to supakov/wview. The
versions after 0.27.0 (WinWeaver 0.28.0 to 0.36.0, wview 0.2.0 to 0.7.0) have no part on the
page yet: a page for them comes when the customer asks for an acceptance of them.
