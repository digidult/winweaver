# WinWeaver 0.1.57

The runs of CI keep their artifacts a week, within the free storage of GitHub Actions.

## What changed

- **Artifacts kept 7 days.** Every upload of `ci.yml` sets `retention-days`: the archives, the
  screenshots, the presentation and the browser edition 7 days, `ci-files` (the lock file and the
  format patch) 3 days. Before, they stayed the 90 days of the repository: the runs of a busy week in
  the mirror kept about 740 MB, above the 0.5 GB a month of the free plan. A release publishes its zips
  in its own run, to the Releases, which do not count against that storage.
