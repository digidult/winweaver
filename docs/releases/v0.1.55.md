# WinWeaver 0.1.55

The search of every tab is full: a rule is found by any of its fields.

## What changed

- **The profile search** finds a rule by every field of the catalog, in English and in the language of
  the interface: its id, title, description and the texts of its description, its search words, its
  parameters and the titles of their values, the titles of its groups, and what it writes or runs:
  registry keys in every form (`HKLM:\X`, `HKLM\X`, `HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\X`), value names, values (a
  number in decimal and in hex) and types, the settings and commands of the answer file, script lines,
  services, features, capabilities, apps and programs. The values the profile gives a rule count too.
- **The other tabs**: a catalog tab, a comparison of catalogs, the differences and the bookmarks search
  the rules they name the same way; the editor of a catalog gets a search of its tree; a registry file
  finds a key in every form and a value by its type too; a Group Policy file by the type of a value; a
  users file by every field of an account but its password; the MCP tool `search_rules` by every field.
- The text of every rule for the search is made once a catalog and a language, so a search through
  thousands of imported policies stays quick.
