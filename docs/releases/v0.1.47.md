# WinWeaver 0.1.47

The MCP server of WinWeaver on the one of W# (the list of 09.11.2026, the second, item 2).

## What changed

- The server, its protocol, its transports, its journal, its log, its tab and its command line are
  the ones of W# 0.1.48 (`wwmcp`); `winweaver-mcp` keeps the tools of WinWeaver alone, a source of
  tools named `winweaver` with its workspace of profiles, its 36 tools in six groups (profiles,
  rules, bookmarks, catalogs, registry, this PC) and its resources.
- The MCP tab: **Tools** turns any tool, a group of them or all of WinWeaver off for the clients,
  and holds the options of WinWeaver (the registry, reading this PC, changing the bookmarks and the
  catalogs, the profile at the start), which were checkboxes of the card of the server before;
  **Call a tool** runs any tool by its name with its arguments and shows the answer; **Open the
  log** shows the log of today. The modes read: nothing changes; edit: what the program holds
  changes in memory; files: files are written too.
- The configuration file `.wwmcp` takes the format of W#: the options of WinWeaver and the switches
  of its tools in its branch `plugins.winweaver`. A file of WinWeaver 0.1.46 or earlier is written
  again in the new format when the tab opens it or `winweaver --mcp` starts with it. The command
  line is the same (`--read-pc`, `--registry`, `--edit-library`, `--profile` are the options of the
  source by their keys) with `--off winweaver/TOOL` and `--set winweaver.KEY=VALUE`.
- The texts of the tab of W# translated into every built-in language; the guide in nine languages
  tells the new tab.
