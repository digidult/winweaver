# WinWeaver 0.1.46

WinWeaver on W# 0.1.47, the framework as an SDK of its own (the list of 09.11.2026, item 3).

## What changed

- The texts W# 0.1.47 adds to the window are translated into every built-in language: the hint
  of the search of a page of embedded files, a page that is not one, the name of a text without a
  file, a text too large or not of UTF-8.
- W# brings embedded files (`ww::embedded`, `ww::embed!`): a set of files of the executable found
  by its path in the language of the interface, its pages opened in tabs of the window with their
  links inside the set; the pages of WinWeaver may move onto it later.
