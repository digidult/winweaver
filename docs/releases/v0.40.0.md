# WinWeaver 0.40.0

The programs of WinWeaver are those of 0.39.0; wview opens a text of any size in parts (wview
0.11.0).

## What is new

- wview:
  - A text larger than **Largest file read whole** of the settings (64 MB at first; it was the
    largest file to open, and a larger one stayed closed) opens in parts: its lines are counted
    in a thread while the view shows the start, with their numbers on the left; the wheel, the
    arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Home, End and a bar of its own move through it to its last
    line, whatever its size, and Left and Right move sideways.
  - The search of such a text lists the lines that hold every word (at most 10000), found while
    the view stays at work, and a click on one goes to it in the text, marked.
  - A view inside a view has no hole any more between its line and its view.
  - The guide in nine languages tells the large texts.
