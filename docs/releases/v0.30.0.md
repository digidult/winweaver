# WinWeaver 0.30.0

The customer's list of 30.10.2026, for the first testers outside Ukraine.

## What is new

- A preset opens with the language of the window (or of Windows) and the region of the user's
  Windows, and with the input languages of this Windows; English (United States) and the layout
  United States-International where nothing fits. The presets were Ukrainian before.
- The form Profile, first in the tree, edits the name, the author and the comment of a profile.
  The view of the profile (the first part of the navigation row) leads to every form.
- The menu This PC is in the main menu too, so a read of this PC into a new profile needs no open
  profile; its items are on only where they apply to the selection in the tree.
- The coloured stripe of a part of the description stands inside its card and is a setting of
  General; the tint of the card stays.

## Fixed

- A window maximized at the last close blinked at the start: the fit to the monitor sent it a
  size before its state was known. It opens maximized at once now.
- The browser edition: the profile tab shows the disc of 0.29.0 there too (nothing to change).
