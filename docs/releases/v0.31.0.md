# WinWeaver 0.31.0

The programs of WinWeaver are those of 0.30.3; W# gets the translations of plugins, and wview, the
viewer of files with plugins, becomes a program of the repository (wview 0.2.0, phase 2, part 1).

## What is new

- W#: `ww::i18n::add` adds the translations of a plugin to the ones a program embeds, under the
  files of the folder `lang`; the language shown takes them at once.
- wview (`wview/`, a workspace of its own):
  - The interface of the native plugins has a version of its own (1): a plugin opens a file with
    its settings, gives its translations, draws its settings in Settings, Plugins, and a view asks
    the window for a step through the files of its folder.
  - The keys and the mouse of a view follow one set of rules: a click gives the view the keys it
    names, Tab and Escape give them back, the shortcuts of the window stay the window's.
  - Images: the wheel zooms at the pointer, a drag moves the image with the hand, a double click
    goes between the fit and 100%, the arrows, the page keys, Home and End step through the
    folder, + and - zoom, 0 fits, 1 shows 100%. Settings: smooth pixels above 100%, the largest
    side of a decoded image.
  - Text and Markdown: a setting of the largest file to open.
  - The window, the plugins and the guide in the eight languages of WinWeaver.
