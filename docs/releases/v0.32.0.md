# WinWeaver 0.32.0

The programs of WinWeaver are those of 0.31.0; wview, the viewer of files with plugins, shows
images whole (wview 0.3.0, phase 3).

## What is new

- wview, the plugin of images:
  - An image is decoded in a thread of its own: a small one opens with its tab, a larger one
    opens with Decoding... and shows when it is there. It is decoded whole up to the limit of the
    settings (64, 256 or 1024 megapixels), a larger one smaller, and the status bar says so.
  - A large image is drawn in parts at the detail the zoom needs (its levels, each half of the
    one before, in tiles of 1024 pixels), so that 100% of a picture of 4000 x 2500 is as sharp as
    its fit is smooth.
  - A GIF plays its frames drawn on its canvas; Space plays and pauses it, the comma and the full
    stop step through its frames, and the bar has Play, Previous frame and Next frame. A TIFF or
    an ICO of several pages turns them with the same keys and Previous page and Next page; an
    icon opens at its largest frame.
  - A photo is turned the way its camera was held (the orientation of EXIF).
  - + and - zoom by steps, 1 to 4 show 100% to 400%, and the arrows move an image larger than the
    window.
  - Settings: the orientation of EXIF, an animation played when it opens, the largest image
    decoded whole; the eight languages and the guide say so.
  - The tests make their files (a BMP, a GIF of three frames, a TIFF of two pages with an
    orientation) and check the frames, the pages, the orientation and the tiles; the screenshots
    `v6-` to `v8-` show an animation and a large image fitted and at 100%.
- wwrt reads the metadata of WIC (the features of `windows` for a PROPVARIANT).
