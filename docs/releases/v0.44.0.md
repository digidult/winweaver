# WinWeaver 0.44.0

The repository is split in three (the decision of the customer of 07.11.2026); the programs of
WinWeaver and wwlite are those of 0.43.0.

## What is new

- W#, the framework of the window (`ww-base`, `ww`), and the system of the plugins (`wwrt`, the
  stable interface and its crate, the examples wv-hello and wv-mandelbrot) are in
  [supakov/ww](https://github.com/supakov/ww), W# 0.44.0.
- wview with its plugins and the text editor wedit are in
  [supakov/wview](https://github.com/supakov/wview), wview 0.15.0 and wedit 0.44.0, with their own
  CI and archives.
- WinWeaver takes `ww` and `ww-base` as a git dependency of the branch `main` of supakov/ww
  (`[workspace.dependencies]`); cargo fetches the private repository with git
  (`net.git-fetch-with-cli`), CI with the token of the secret `WW_TOKEN`, and both take the last
  commit of `main` before a build (`cargo update -p ww -p ww-base`). The test of the translations
  reads the sources of W# at `ww::SOURCES`, its checkout.
- CI and `build-all.cmd` build WinWeaver, wwlite and the browser edition alone: the job of wview,
  the archive of wedit and the option `nowview` left; `build-all.cmd` takes W# of main first.
- The documents of W# (`docs/ww.md`, `docs/plugins.md`) and of wview (`docs/todo/wview.md`) went
  with their code; this repository keeps the history of all three until 0.43.0.
