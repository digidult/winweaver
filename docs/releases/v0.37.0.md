# WinWeaver 0.37.0

The programs of WinWeaver are those of 0.36.1; wview gets the interface 2 of its native plugins:
MIME types, items of the menu, bytes in memory and views inside a view (wview 0.8.0), item 3 of
the list of the customer of 05.11.2026.

## What is new

- wview:
  - **Content** in the bar of a binary file: when the node selected holds a file of a type another
    plugin shows, the right side shows it with that plugin, with its own buttons over it, and
    follows the selection. The manifest of a program shows as a text, its icons, cursors and
    bitmaps as images, its HTML pages as texts, a picture, a text or another program among its
    data by its first bytes. **Tree and bytes** goes back.
  - The menu of the window holds the items of the tab in front and a submenu of every plugin with
    items of its own. Binary files: **Open the content in a tab** (a tab of the bytes, without a
    file), **Save the node as a file** (an icon as an ICO file, else its bytes), **Read every
    node**; the plugin of binary structures: **Folder of the descriptions** (the folder
    `plugins\formats` in Explorer).
  - Texts: XML files (`.xml`, `.manifest`), and texts of UTF-16 by their mark.
  - The page of the plugins names the MIME types of every format.
  - The guide in nine languages tells the menu, XML and the content of a node.
- For the authors of plugins (the SDK, `docs/plugins.md` section 6.1):
  - The native interface 2: the MIME types of a format (`Format::mimes`), `Plugin::open_bytes`
    of bytes in memory, `Plugin::menu` and `act`, `View::menu`, `View::act` with the `Host`, the
    requests `Status`, `Open` and `OpenBytes`, and views inside a view (`Host::opens`,
    `Host::open_inside`, `wwrt::inner::Inner`, at most 4 deep). `wwrt::mime` names the type of
    some bytes by their first ones and the spellings of a type; `BlockFile::from_bytes` reads
    bytes in memory. A plugin of the interface 1 is refused by its build and is built again.
  - The template `wv-hello` has every new part: a MIME type, bytes in memory, an item of the
    plugin and two of its view.
  - Descriptions of formats (`.wwformat`): `mimes`, the types of the files of a format, and
    `content`, the type of the bytes of a field, with three forms of the resources of Windows
    (`image/x-dib`, `image/x-dib-icon`, `image/x-dib-cursor`) made files of their type.
  - The stable interface (`wview.h`) stays at version 1.
- W#: `widgets::side_panel` takes an `Id`, so a panel takes the place of its view.
