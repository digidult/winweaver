# WinWeaver 0.36.1

The programs are those of 0.36.0 (wview 0.7.0); the documentation and CI change.

## What is new

- The documents hold the current state, the history is in `docs/releases/` and the plans in
  `docs/todo/` (a file a subject, cleaned as its tasks are done): the acceptance waiting, what stays
  to come of wview, and the design of plugins with MIME types, menu items and other plugins inside
  their view, for the approval of the customer.
- `AGENTS.md` is the map and the rules alone: the facts every change of a part must respect moved
  to `docs/spec/05-details.md` by part, the tree of the folders and the modules to
  `docs/spec/04-architecture.md`; links lead to the information that changes. `docs/plugins.md`
  describes wview as it is.
- CI: a run on demand needs the word of the user and builds the parts of its task alone: the new
  switches `checks` (the Linux job left out, its checks having run on the computer of the
  developer) and `package` (the release builds, the archives and their checks left out, for the
  tests and the screenshots).
