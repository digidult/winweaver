# WinWeaver 0.36.0

The programs of WinWeaver are those of 0.35.0; wview gets the stable interface for plugins in
other languages and its example in C, the Mandelbrot set (wview 0.7.0, phase 7).

## What is new

- wview:
  - The stable interface of the plugins, `wview.h` (version 1): a DLL in any language with a C FFI
    that exports `wv_plugin_get` draws a picture and names its controls, and wview draws them over
    the view and gives it the keys and the mouse. Such a plugin loads into every later release
    without being built again; the SDK carries the header and its README.
  - Its example, the Mandelbrot set in C (`plugins\wv_mandelbrot.dll`, `examples\seahorse.mandel`):
    Iterations, Palette, Smooth colours, Zoom in, Zoom out, Reset and Save over the picture; the
    wheel zooms at the pointer, a drag moves the picture, Shift and a drag zoom into a rectangle, a
    double click centres and zooms in, the right button zooms out; the arrows, + and -, Page Up and
    Page Down, P, Home and Escape. The picture comes in steps, coarse at once and finer after.
  - The guide in nine languages has the section The Mandelbrot set.
  - CI builds the example with the compiler of Visual Studio, draws it in the screenshot `v13-`
    and checks that the release opens and draws it; `build-all.cmd` builds it where Visual Studio
    is installed.
