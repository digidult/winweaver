# WinWeaver 0.28.0

The customer's list of 28.10.2026: groups removed in the catalog editor, a lighter menu and the
list of regions in the browser, the build of the browser edition by hand, every catalog of the
folder `imports` in wwlite, a flatter tree of the built-in catalog, the whole description of a
rule in the language of the window.

## What is new

- The catalog editor removes a group: select it, Remove the group, and after a question it goes
  with every group and policy under it and their texts in other languages. The top group of the
  file stays.
- The browser edition of wwlite has no Open recent and no Exit in its menu (a page keeps no paths
  and cannot close its tab), and the field of the user region (`default-user.region`) has its list
  of regions there too: 249 regions of a Windows 11 with English names, embedded in the browser
  build alone (`data/resources/regions.json`); on Windows the list still comes from the system.
- `docs/wasm.md` has the steps to build the browser edition by hand, without CI.
- wwlite for Windows reads every catalog file of the folder `imports` next to it at the start, as
  its guide said; the user puts in or takes out the files to choose the catalogs. The browser
  edition reads none.
- The built-in catalog is flatter: under Other the rules of security, network, logging,
  telemetry and system stand directly beside the group of the apps; the network and the logging
  of PowerShell are part of Security; Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome and Brave are groups of Apps
  under Policies. The ids of the rules are the same, so profiles do not change.

## Fixed

- The description of a rule was partly in English in the other languages: the effect under What
  it does, the risk, the versions, the check and the rollback, the titles of the values of a
  parameter, and the translated search words were not used. They come from the translation now;
  every built-in language had them.
