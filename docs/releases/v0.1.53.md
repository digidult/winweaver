# WinWeaver 0.1.53

A catalog of the user has every feature of the built-in catalog, and its editor edits all of it.

## What changed

- **A catalog file holds rules of every kind.** A policy of a catalog of the user (an import or one made
  in the editor) is a rule of the built-in catalog: every field (the texts of the description, the
  level, on by default, the search words, the rules it needs and conflicts with, built-in ones too) and
  every kind of action: registry values of any type, their removals and lists, settings, elements of
  OOBE and commands of the answer file, lines of the script, services, features, capabilities, apps and
  programs, in every phase. Its groups sit under any root of the tree: Installation, Policies or Other.
- **One implementation.** A catalog file is checked with the checks of the built-in catalog (the
  mechanism of a rule and the root of its group, the parameters, the placeholders) and its own (safe
  keys and texts). Its translations have the form of the translation files of the catalog, so every
  text of a policy is translated, its parameters and their values too, and the tab of a catalog shows a
  policy with the cards of the profile, in the language of the interface. Files of the earlier version
  are still read, and a save writes version 2.
- **The editor edits everything**: the form of a policy has its title, description, group, phase, level,
  Details, parameters (a choice, a number, a text, a list), actions of every kind, moved up and down, and
  Translations, one language at a time with the English text as the hint of an empty field; the form of
  a group its texts, its place and its translations; the form of the file its name.
- **Code asks first.** A catalog whose rules run code of their own (scripts, commands, programs,
  settings of the answer file) is imported only after a question that names how many rules do so; the
  command line needs `--code allow` for it, and the MCP tool `set_catalog_policy` never writes such a
  policy. The MCP tools read and write the translations of a policy (`texts`).
